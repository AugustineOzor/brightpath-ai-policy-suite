## 1. Purpose

BrightPath wants colleagues to use AI to work smarter and serve customers better. This policy sets out how to do that safely, lawfully and in line with our values, protecting customers, colleagues, suppliers and the business.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, agency workers and third parties acting on BrightPath's behalf ("workers") who use any AI tool for BrightPath work — on company or personal devices, and whether the tool is standalone (e.g., a chatbot) or embedded in other software (e.g., AI features in office or design tools).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI tool** | Any software that generates content, predictions, recommendations or decisions using machine learning or generative models |
| **Approved AI tool** | A tool listed on the AI Tool Register with status *Approved* |
| **Enterprise GenAI** | BrightPath's licensed generative AI tenant with contractual data-protection terms, no training on our data, and SSO |
| **AI output** | Any text, image, code, analysis or decision produced by an AI tool |
| **Human review** | A competent person checks the output for accuracy, appropriateness and compliance before it is relied on |

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Approved tools
1. Workers **MUST** use only AI tools listed as *Approved* on the AI Tool Register for BrightPath work.
2. Workers **MUST** access approved tools using BrightPath credentials via SSO, never personal accounts.
3. Workers wanting a new tool **MUST** request it through the IT Service Portal (AI Tool Request). Tools **MUST NOT** be purchased on purchasing cards or expensed without an AI Tool Register reference.
4. Workers **MUST NOT** install AI browser extensions, plug-ins, add-ins or meeting bots unless approved.

### 4.2 Data protection
5. Workers **MUST** follow the [Generative AI Data Policy](Generative-AI-Data-Policy.md) when entering any data into an AI tool.
6. Workers **MUST NOT** enter Restricted data (e.g., payment card data, colleague health or HR case data, credentials) into any AI tool unless the specific tool and use case are approved for Restricted data.

### 4.3 Responsibility for outputs
7. Workers remain **accountable for any output they use**. AI output **MUST** receive human review before it is published, sent externally, used in a decision about a person, or committed to production code.
8. Workers **MUST** verify facts, figures, legal or regulatory statements, and product claims generated by AI against authoritative sources.
9. Customer-facing content (product descriptions, marketing, ads, customer responses) generated with AI **MUST** comply with the Marketing Code, advertising standards and consumer-protection law; product claims (e.g., "organic", "sustainable", allergen information) **MUST NOT** rely on AI output alone.
10. AI-generated images of people or realistic scenes used externally **MUST** follow the Brand AI Imagery Guidelines and **SHOULD** be labelled where they could mislead.

### 4.4 Prohibited uses
Workers **MUST NOT** use AI tools to:

| # | Prohibited use | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Make or materially determine decisions about recruitment, promotion, discipline, dismissal, scheduling or pay without an approved, assessed system and human decision-maker | Equality Act, UK GDPR Art. 22, EU AI Act Annex III(4) |
| P2 | Monitor, profile or infer emotions of colleagues or customers outside approved systems | Privacy, EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) |
| P3 | Generate content that is discriminatory, harassing, defamatory, sexually explicit or deceptive | Code of Conduct |
| P4 | Impersonate a real person, create deepfakes, or misrepresent AI content as human-created where that would deceive | Consumer protection, trust |
| P5 | Circumvent security controls, generate malicious code or test systems without authorisation | InfoSec Policy |
| P6 | Reproduce third-party copyrighted material, competitor content or supplier IP for commercial use | IP law, supplier contracts |
| P7 | Set or recommend prices in coordination with, or using non-public data about, competitors | Competition law |
| P8 | Provide customers with medical, legal, financial or allergen advice | Customer safety, liability |

### 4.5 Transparency
11. Customers **MUST** be told when they are interacting with an AI system (e.g., chatbots) unless obvious from context.
12. Workers **SHOULD** disclose material AI assistance to colleagues relying on their work where it affects how the work should be checked.

### 4.6 Reporting
13. Workers **MUST** report suspected AI incidents — data entered into the wrong tool, harmful or biased outputs, customer complaints about AI, unexpected AI actions — to the IT Service Desk (category: *AI Incident*) within **24 hours**.
14. Good-faith reports will not result in disciplinary action for the reporter's own inadvertent error.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workers | Comply; complete AI e-learning; report incidents |
| Line managers | Ensure team awareness; approve team use cases within approved tools |
| CIO (Policy owner) | Maintain policy and AI Tool Register |
| CISO | Technical enforcement (CASB, DLP, extension control); incident response |
| DPO | Privacy guidance; DPIA requirements |
| Head of AI Governance | Policy suite coherence; exceptions; reporting to Risk Committee |
| L&D | Mandatory AI literacy training (annual) |

## 6. Training
All workers **MUST** complete *AI at BrightPath* e-learning within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Workers using AI in customer-facing, HR or pricing roles **MUST** complete the role-based module.

## 7. Monitoring & Enforcement
BrightPath monitors use of AI services at application level through security tooling (CASB, DLP, endpoint management) in line with the Colleague Privacy Notice. Breaches may lead to access removal and disciplinary action under the Disciplinary Policy, proportionate to intent and impact.

## 8. Exceptions
Exceptions **MUST** be requested via the Head of AI Governance, include a risk justification and compensating controls, be time-bound (max 12 months) and be recorded in the Exception Register.

## 9. Quick Reference — Can I…?

| Scenario | Answer |
|---|---|
| Use Enterprise GenAI to draft a store team email? | ✅ Yes — review before sending |
| Paste a customer complaint with name and address into a public chatbot? | ❌ No — use Enterprise GenAI and remove identifiers |
| Use AI to shortlist CVs? | ❌ No — only via the approved, assessed recruitment system |
| Generate a product description for the website? | ✅ Yes — verify every product claim |
| Install a free AI note-taker for supplier calls? | ❌ No — use the approved transcription feature |
| Ask AI to write a SQL query against sales data? | ✅ Yes, in approved tools — test before running in production |
